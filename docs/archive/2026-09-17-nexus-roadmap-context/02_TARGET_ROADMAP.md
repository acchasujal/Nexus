# NEXUS — Target Product Roadmap

## Product position

NEXUS should evolve from retrospective criminal-network analysis into:

> **Proactive, evidence-grounded criminal-network intelligence**

Operational loop:

`observe → connect → detect change → forecast only what is justified → alert → verify → act → learn`

## Three headline differentiators

### 1. Early-Warning Network Intelligence
Detect meaningful changes in observed networks and produce evidence-supported forecasts of narrowly defined investigation-relevant next states.

### 2. Digital Shadow + Identity/Network Drift
Fuse authorized/public digital signals with offline evidence to detect identifier changes, emerging relationships and network adaptation.

### 3. Trusted Intelligence Exchange
Combine provenance, SHA-256 integrity, RBAC/audit and future selective trust/federation mechanisms to move verified intelligence across investigations or jurisdictions.

## Supporting capabilities

- Evidence Gap & Contradiction Engine
- Next Best Verification
- Network Pulse / Network Change Detection
- Cross-Branch Intelligence Pulse
- Case DNA / Investigative Memory
- Human-in-the-loop Entity Resolution
- Temporal intelligence and network adaptation detection
- Selective SHA-256 evidence integrity and optional trust anchoring

## Explicit red-team boundaries

Reject:
- person-level future-crime prediction
- guilt/criminality probability
- generic high-risk-suspect labels
- raw investigative data on public blockchains
- naive SHA-256(identifier) as production PSI
- private-account scraping/covert social surveillance
- biometric recognition as a core feature without full governance/evaluation
- generic chatbot as USP
- unsupported algorithms that do not change an investigator decision
- synthetic accuracy presented as field accuracy

Deprioritize:
- additional graph algorithms without decision value
- generic crime heatmaps unless tied to network/temporal change

Vision/research:
- PSI/MPC privacy-preserving deconfliction
- selective Merkle/permissioned-ledger anchoring
- advanced temporal GNN/cryptographic intelligence/biometrics
